## International Genres

### Anime
**URL:** https://geoleap.app/genres/anime
**Category:** International

Anime has grown from a niche hobby into one of the most-watched content categories globally. Crunchyroll hosts the largest anime library with simulcast streaming within hours of Japan broadcast.

**Best platforms:**
| Platform | Library | Strengths |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Crunchyroll | 2,000+ series | Largest catalog, simulcast, manga reader |
| Netflix | 500+ titles | Studio Ghibli, exclusive originals, dubs |
| HIDIVE | 400+ series | Older catalog, niche titles |
| Amazon Prime Video | 200+ series | Some exclusives, dubs |

**Viewing stats:** Anime accounts for approximately 8-9% of Netflix global viewing hours.

**Trending titles:** One Piece, Demon Slayer, Jujutsu Kaisen, My Hero Academia, Attack on Titan

**Best countries:** Japan (deepest local catalog), US (Crunchyroll + Netflix), UK (Crunchyroll + Netflix)
