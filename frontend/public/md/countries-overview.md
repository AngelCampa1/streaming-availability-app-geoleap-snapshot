# GeoLeap - Streaming by Country

> Streaming platform availability and landscape across 57 countries. GeoLeap helps users discover which streaming platforms and content are available in each country.

## Key Takeaways
- GeoLeap covers streaming availability in 57 countries across 6 regions
- Each country guide includes available platforms, local pricing, and regional alternatives
- Streaming prices vary up to 19x between countries for the same service
- The US has the most platforms (20+) while emerging markets often have unique local services

---

## North America (3 Countries)

### United States (US)
- **Currency:** USD
- **Available Platforms:** 20
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, HBO Max, Amazon Prime Video
- **Landscape:** The most developed streaming market globally. Home to all major global platforms plus numerous niche services. US Netflix carries the largest content library worldwide. Hulu remains a US-exclusive service, making it a top priority for American travelers using GeoLeap.

### Canada (CA)
- **Currency:** CAD
- **Available Platforms:** 12
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Crunchyroll
- **Landscape:** Strong overlap with US platforms but with distinct licensing differences. Crave (Bell Media) is the dominant local platform carrying HBO and Showtime content. CBC Gem offers free Canadian programming. Canadian Netflix has a smaller library than the US version.

### Mexico (MX)
- **Currency:** MXN
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, Crunchyroll
- **Landscape:** Growing streaming market with strong telco-bundled offerings. Blim TV (Televisa) is the largest local platform. ViX (TelevisaUnivision) serves the Spanish-language market. Competitive pricing compared to North American neighbors.

## Europe (15 Countries)

### United Kingdom (GB)
- **Currency:** GBP
- **Available Platforms:** 15
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, BritBox
- **Landscape:** One of Europe's most competitive streaming markets. BBC iPlayer and ITVX are free ad-supported services with strong original content. BritBox aggregates classic British programming. Sky (via NOW TV) remains a premium option for sports and movies. Channel 4 streaming is free with registration.

### Germany (DE)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 13
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, WOW (Sky)
- **Landscape:** Largest streaming market in continental Europe. MagentaTV (Deutsche Telekom) bundles live TV with streaming. Joyn is the free ad-supported platform from ProSiebenSat.1 and Discovery. RTL+ is growing with local originals. Part of the DACH (Germany-Austria-Switzerland) content region.

### France (FR)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 8
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, MyCANAL
- **Landscape:** Unique market shaped by strict media chronology regulations - new theatrical releases have a 15-month window before appearing on SVOD platforms. MyCANAL (Canal+) is the dominant local platform with premium content rights. France has lower streaming adoption compared to UK/Germany partly due to these regulations. Salto (joint venture) was shut down in 2023.

### Spain (ES)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 12
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, Apple TV+
- **Landscape:** Rapidly growing market with strong telco integration. Movistar+ (Telefonica) is the dominant local platform bundling live TV, sports, and streaming. Spain produces significant original content for global platforms, particularly Netflix. La Liga streaming rights are a key differentiator among services.

### Italy (IT)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 13
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, NOW (Sky)
- **Landscape:** Growing streaming adoption with strong free-to-air tradition. RaiPlay is the free public broadcaster platform with a large Italian content library. DAZN holds exclusive Serie A rights. NOW (Sky Italia) offers premium movies and sports. Italian original content production is increasing across platforms.

### Netherlands (NL)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 10
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Videoland
- **Landscape:** High internet penetration drives strong streaming adoption. Videoland (RTL Nederland) is the leading local platform with Dutch originals and reality content. NPO Start is the free public broadcaster service. Dutch audiences are highly proficient in English, boosting international content consumption.

### Sweden (SE)
- **Currency:** SEK
- **Available Platforms:** 13
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Viaplay
- **Landscape:** Highly digital Nordic market with early streaming adoption. Viaplay (NENT Group) is the dominant Nordic platform with sports rights and Scandinavian originals. SVT Play is the free public broadcaster. TV4 Play offers ad-supported content. Sweden is a hub for Nordic noir and crime drama production.

### Norway (NO)
- **Currency:** NOK
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Viaplay
- **Landscape:** One of the highest GDP per capita countries, supporting premium streaming subscriptions. Viaplay dominates for sports including Premier League rights. NRK TV is the free public broadcaster with a strong content library. TV 2 Play offers news, sports, and entertainment.

### Denmark (DK)
- **Currency:** DKK
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Viaplay
- **Landscape:** Mature Nordic streaming market. DR TV is the free public broadcaster known for quality Danish drama. TV 2 Play is a strong local competitor. Viaplay holds key sports rights. Denmark has a strong tradition of producing internationally acclaimed TV series.

### Finland (FI)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Viaplay
- **Landscape:** Tech-savvy market with high broadband penetration. Yle Areena is the free public broadcaster platform with Finnish and Swedish-language content. MTV Katsomo and Ruutu offer local programming. C More provides premium sports and movies in the Nordic region.

### Switzerland (CH)
- **Currency:** CHF
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Blue TV
- **Landscape:** Unique multilingual market serving German, French, and Italian-speaking regions. Content libraries often vary by language region. Blue TV (Swisscom) is the dominant telco-bundled platform. SRF, RTS, and RSI provide free public broadcasting in each language. Premium pricing reflects high purchasing power.

### Austria (AT)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 11
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, WOW (Sky)
- **Landscape:** Part of the DACH market sharing content with Germany and Switzerland. ORF ON is the free public broadcaster platform. Joyn and RTL+ serve the German-language market. ServusTV provides free content including Formula 1 coverage. Smaller market benefits from German-language content scale.

### Poland (PL)
- **Currency:** PLN
- **Available Platforms:** 8
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, HBO Max
- **Landscape:** Fastest-growing streaming market in Central Europe. Player (TVN/Warner Bros. Discovery) is a leading local platform. Polsat Box Go (Polsat/Cyfrowy Polsat) bundles streaming with traditional TV. Polish originals are gaining international attention. Competitive pricing compared to Western Europe.

### Portugal (PT)
- **Currency:** EUR
- **Available Platforms:** 8
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, HBO Max
- **Landscape:** Growing market with strong telco integration. RTP Play is the free public broadcaster. NOS and MEO bundle streaming with telecom services. Portuguese-language content connects to the broader Lusophone market including Brazil. Sports streaming (particularly football) drives subscriber growth.

### Turkey (TR)
- **Currency:** TRY
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, BluTV, Gain
- **Landscape:** Major content production hub - Turkish dizis (drama series) are exported globally. BluTV is the leading local subscription platform with Turkish originals. Gain (Demiroren Media) offers free ad-supported content. Turkey produces more TV drama hours than any country except the US. Favorable exchange rates make subscriptions extremely affordable.

## Asia (6 Countries)

### Japan (JP)
- **Currency:** JPY
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Crunchyroll, Hulu
- **Landscape:** Unique market where anime dominates streaming consumption. U-NEXT is the largest local platform with over 300,000 titles. AbemaTV offers free ad-supported content including anime and variety shows. Hulu Japan (operated by Nippon TV) is distinct from US Hulu. Japan is the world's largest anime production market.

### South Korea (KR)
- **Currency:** KRW
- **Available Platforms:** 6
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Wavve
- **Landscape:** K-content powerhouse accounting for 8-9% of Netflix's global viewing hours. Wavve (SK Telecom/KBS/MBC) and TVING (CJ ENM) are dominant local platforms. Korean originals command premium production budgets. Coupang Play is growing via e-commerce integration. The market produces globally popular K-drama and K-variety content.

### India (IN)
- **Currency:** INR
- **Available Platforms:** 6
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar, Apple TV+, JioCinema
- **Landscape:** The world's cheapest streaming market with plans starting under $2/month. JioCinema (Viacom18/Reliance) disrupted the market by offering IPL cricket streaming free. Disney+ Hotstar is a dominant force combining Disney content with cricket and Bollywood. Regional language content across Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and more drives massive adoption. Mobile-first consumption patterns shape platform strategies.

### Singapore (SG)
- **Currency:** SGD
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, HBO Go
- **Landscape:** Affluent, tech-savvy market with high streaming adoption. MeWatch (Mediacorp) is the free local platform with Singaporean and Asian content. Viu serves pan-Asian content. Multilingual market (English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil) creates diverse content demand. Strong broadband infrastructure supports high-quality streaming.

### Hong Kong (HK)
- **Currency:** HKD
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Viu
- **Landscape:** Cantonese and English-language market. ViuTV (PCCW) offers free local content. myTV SUPER provides Cantonese drama and variety shows. Strong demand for both Western and Asian content. High smartphone penetration drives mobile streaming.

### Taiwan (TW)
- **Currency:** TWD
- **Available Platforms:** 6
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, KKTV
- **Landscape:** Mandarin-language market with strong demand for Korean and Japanese content. KKTV specializes in Asian drama content. LineTV integrates with the dominant messaging platform LINE. FriDay Video (Far EasTone) bundles with telecom services. Growing local content production for both domestic and international audiences.

## South America (4 Countries)

### Brazil (BR)
- **Currency:** BRL
- **Available Platforms:** 10
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max, Apple TV+
- **Landscape:** Largest streaming market in Latin America. Globoplay (Grupo Globo) is the dominant local platform leveraging Brazil's massive telenovela production ecosystem. Brazil is Netflix's third-largest market globally. Portuguese-language content creates a distinct market from Spanish-speaking neighbors. Strong sports streaming demand, particularly football.

### Argentina (AR)
- **Currency:** ARS
- **Available Platforms:** 10
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max, Apple TV+
- **Landscape:** Price-sensitive market affected by currency fluctuations and inflation. Argentine audiences historically benefited from favorable exchange rates for international subscriptions. Strong local content production tradition. Flow (Telecom Argentina) bundles streaming with telecom services. Football streaming rights are a major competitive factor.

### Colombia (CO)
- **Currency:** COP
- **Available Platforms:** 8
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, Apple TV+
- **Landscape:** Growing streaming market with strong telco bundle adoption. Claro Video and Movistar Play bundle with mobile plans. Colombian original productions (particularly narco-dramas) have gained global audiences. Caracol Play offers local content. Mobile-first market with improving broadband infrastructure.

### Chile (CL)
- **Currency:** CLP
- **Available Platforms:** 9
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, HBO Max, Apple TV+
- **Landscape:** Highest GDP per capita in Latin America, supporting premium streaming adoption. Most stable streaming market in the region. Strong broadband infrastructure with high fixed-line penetration. Movistar Play and Entel bundles drive telco-integrated streaming. Chilean audiences consume both local and international content.

## Oceania (2 Countries)

### Australia (AU)
- **Currency:** AUD
- **Available Platforms:** 14
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Stan
- **Landscape:** Highly competitive market with strong local platforms. Stan (Nine Entertainment) is the leading local service with premium content deals including Paramount. Binge (Foxtel) offers HBO and Warner Bros. content. Kayo Sports is the dominant sports streaming platform. 7plus, 9Now, and 10 Play provide free ad-supported content. Foxtel NOW offers premium packages.

### New Zealand (NZ)
- **Currency:** NZD
- **Available Platforms:** 9
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Neon
- **Landscape:** Smaller market that often shares content deals with Australia. TVNZ+ is the free public broadcaster platform. Neon (Sky NZ) carries HBO and premium content. Sky Sport NOW provides sports streaming. Three Now offers free ad-supported content. New Zealand has growing local production, boosted by international film and TV shoots.

## Africa (1 Country)

### South Africa (ZA)
- **Currency:** ZAR
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, Apple TV+, Showmax
- **Landscape:** Leading streaming market on the African continent. Showmax (MultiChoice/Comcast) is the dominant local platform with Afrikaans and multilingual African content, plus Premier League rights. DStv (MultiChoice) transitioned from satellite to streaming. Data costs remain a barrier for mass adoption. Growing Nollywood and local content production feeds platform demand.

## Middle East (2 Countries)

### United Arab Emirates (AE)
- **Currency:** AED
- **Available Platforms:** 7
- **Top 5:** Netflix, Disney+, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Shahid
- **Landscape:** Hub for Arabic and international streaming in the Gulf region. Shahid (MBC Group) is the dominant Arabic-language platform with originals, news, and live TV. StarzPlay (now Lionsgate+) serves English and Arabic content. OSN+ offers premium Western content for the MENA region. High purchasing power supports premium subscription tiers. Content is offered in both Arabic and English.
