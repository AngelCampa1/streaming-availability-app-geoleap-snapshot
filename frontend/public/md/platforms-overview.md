# GeoLeap - Streaming Platforms Overview

> Detailed guide to 42 of the major streaming platforms tracked by GeoLeap (out of 42 total), including pricing, availability, features, and content highlights. Designed for quick reference and comparison.
>
> **Note:** "Countries Available" refers to the number of countries where GeoLeap tracks this platform's content, not the platform's total global availability. For example, Netflix operates in 190+ countries but GeoLeap currently tracks content in 57 of them.

## Key Takeaways
- GeoLeap tracks 42 streaming platforms with detailed profiles for 20 major services
- Each platform profile includes pricing, features, content highlights, and country availability
- Pricing ranges from free (Tubi, Pluto TV) to $24.99/month (Netflix Premium)
- Platform availability varies significantly by country
