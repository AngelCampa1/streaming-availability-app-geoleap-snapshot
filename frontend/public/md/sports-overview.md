## Football (Soccer)

### Premier League
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** August - May
- **Global Platforms:** Peacock (US), Optus Sport (AU), DAZN (CA), Viaplay (Nordics), Star Sports / JioStar (India)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £50+/mo via Sky Sports + TNT Sports combined
- **Key Events:** Opening weekend, North London Derby, Manchester Derby, title race run-in, final matchday
- **Notes:** UK viewers need two separate subscriptions (Sky Sports and TNT Sports) for full coverage. Showmax holds rights in South Africa. NBC/Peacock carries all matches in the US.

### UEFA Champions League
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** September - June
- **Global Platforms:** Paramount+ (US), TNT Sports (UK), DAZN (multiple), Stan Sport (AU)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £30.99/mo via TNT Sports / Discovery+
- **Key Events:** Group stage matchdays, Round of 16, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, Final
- **Notes:** DAZN holds rights in multiple European and global markets. CBS/Paramount+ carries all matches in the US. Format expanded to 36-team league phase from 2024-25.

### La Liga
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** August - May
- **Global Platforms:** ESPN+ (US), Premier Sports (UK), DAZN (multiple), Viaplay (Nordics)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** Spain - €50+/mo via Movistar+ / DAZN combined
- **Key Events:** El Clasico (Barcelona vs Real Madrid), Madrid Derby, title deciders
- **Notes:** Rights split between multiple broadcasters in Spain. ESPN+ carries matches in the US at a competitive price point.

### Serie A
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** August - May
- **Global Platforms:** Paramount+ (US), TNT Sports (UK), DAZN (IT), Kayo Sports (AU)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** Italy - €40+/mo via DAZN Italy
- **Key Events:** Derby della Madonnina (AC Milan vs Inter), Derby d'Italia (Juventus vs Inter), Rome Derby
- **Notes:** DAZN holds exclusive rights in Italy. Paramount+ carries matches in the US and select markets.

### Bundesliga
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** August - May
- **Global Platforms:** ESPN+ (US), Sky Sport (DE), DAZN (multiple)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** Germany - €40+/mo via Sky Sport + DAZN combined
- **Key Events:** Der Klassiker (Bayern vs Dortmund), Revierderby, Nordderby, title race
- **Notes:** Rights split between Sky and DAZN in Germany. Free-to-air highlights available on Sportschau (ARD). ESPN+ carries matches in the US.

### Ligue 1
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** August - May
- **Global Platforms:** beIN Sports (US/MENA), DAZN (FR), Amazon Prime Video (FR, select matches)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** France - €40+/mo via DAZN France
- **Key Events:** Le Classique (PSG vs Marseille), Lyon Derby, Cote d'Azur Derby
- **Notes:** DAZN acquired majority French domestic rights. Amazon Prime Video carries select Friday night matches in France. Le Classique is the most-watched domestic fixture.

### MLS (Major League Soccer)
- **Category:** Football
- **Season:** February - November
- **Global Platforms:** MLS Season Pass via Apple TV+ (worldwide exclusive)
- **Cheapest Option:** Global - $14.99/mo via Apple TV+ MLS Season Pass (included with Apple TV+ subscription for select matches)
- **Most Expensive Option:** United States - $14.99/mo standalone MLS Season Pass
- **Key Events:** Opening Day, Rivalry weeks, Leagues Cup, MLS All-Star Game, MLS Cup Playoffs, MLS Cup Final
- **Notes:** Apple TV+ holds exclusive global rights - first league-wide deal with a single streaming platform. All matches available worldwide with no blackouts. Lionel Messi's presence at Inter Miami has driven global viewership.

## American Football

### NFL (National Football League)
- **Category:** American Football
- **Season:** September - February
- **Global Platforms:** NFL+ (US), DAZN (international), ESPN+ (Monday Night Football), Amazon Prime Video (Thursday Night Football), Peacock/Paramount+ (select games)
- **Cheapest Option:** Brazil - ~$8/mo via DAZN
- **Most Expensive Option:** United States - $200+/yr for NFL Sunday Ticket via YouTube TV
- **Key Events:** Opening Night, Thanksgiving games, Christmas games, Wild Card Weekend, Divisional Round, Conference Championships, Super Bowl
- **Notes:** US rights split across multiple networks (CBS, FOX, NBC, ESPN, Amazon). DAZN holds international rights in most markets. NFL Sunday Ticket moved from DirecTV to YouTube TV. Super Bowl is the most-watched annual broadcast in the US.

## Basketball

### NBA (National Basketball Association)
- **Category:** Basketball
- **Season:** October - June
- **Global Platforms:** NBA League Pass (worldwide), ESPN+ (US, select games), TNT Sports (US)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$18/yr via NBA League Pass
- **Most Expensive Option:** United States - ~$200/yr via NBA League Pass (no blackouts tier)
- **Key Events:** Opening Night, Christmas Day games, All-Star Weekend, Play-In Tournament, NBA Playoffs, NBA Finals
- **Notes:** NBA League Pass pricing varies dramatically by country - India is often the cheapest market. US viewers face local blackout restrictions on League Pass. New US media deal (starting 2025-26) includes Amazon Prime Video, NBC, and ESPN. International League Pass remains the best value for full coverage.

## Motorsport

### Formula 1
- **Category:** Motorsport
- **Season:** March - December
- **Global Platforms:** Apple TV (US from 2026), F1 TV Pro (direct in supported markets), Sky Sports F1 (UK), Viaplay (Nordics)
- **Cheapest Option:** Free in select markets - Austria (ORF/ServusTV), Belgium (RTBF/VRT), Switzerland (SRF)
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - typically highest through Sky Sports bundles
- **Key Events:** Bahrain GP (season opener), Monaco GP, British GP, Singapore GP (night race), Abu Dhabi GP (season finale)
- **Notes:** Free-to-air coverage is available in select markets including Austria, Belgium, and Switzerland. F1 TV Pro offers direct streaming with onboard cameras and data in supported countries. In the US, Apple TV is the exclusive F1 broadcaster from 2026. Sky holds exclusive live rights in the UK.

### MotoGP
- **Category:** Motorsport
- **Season:** March - November
- **Global Platforms:** MotoGP VideoPass (direct worldwide), beIN Sports (MENA/select markets), BT Sport/TNT Sports (UK), NBC Sports (US)
- **Cheapest Option:** Global - MotoGP VideoPass at ~$8/mo (with annual plan)
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £30.99/mo via TNT Sports for combined motorsport access
- **Key Events:** Qatar GP (season opener), Spanish GP, Italian GP (Mugello), Valencia GP (season finale)
- **Notes:** MotoGP VideoPass is the official direct-to-consumer platform available worldwide. Includes all classes (MotoGP, Moto2, Moto3). Red Bull TV carries select races for free. Spain and Italy are the largest audience markets.

## Combat Sports

### UFC (Ultimate Fighting Championship)
- **Category:** Mixed Martial Arts
- **Season:** Year-round (numbered events + Fight Nights)
- **Global Platforms:** Paramount+ (US from 2026), DAZN (select markets), UFC Fight Pass (global library and prelims)
- **Cheapest Option:** United States - $8.99/mo via Paramount+ Essential (includes all UFC events)
- **Most Expensive Option:** Previously US - $79.99 per PPV event under the historical ESPN+ model
- **Key Events:** UFC numbered events (monthly), International Fight Week (July), year-end championship bouts
- **Notes:** Beginning in 2026, Paramount+ is the exclusive US home for UFC events, with select marquee fights simulcast on CBS. UFC Fight Pass provides prelim coverage and the full fight library globally. DAZN carries events in select European and Asian markets.

### Boxing
- **Category:** Combat Sports
- **Season:** Year-round
- **Global Platforms:** DAZN (primary global), ESPN+ (US, Top Rank), Showtime/Paramount+ (US), Sky Sports (UK)
- **Cheapest Option:** Global - DAZN at ~$10/mo for most fight cards
- **Most Expensive Option:** United States / United Kingdom - $79.99+ per PPV for premium title fights
- **Key Events:** Undisputed championship bouts, major PPV events, Canelo Alvarez fights, heavyweight title fights
- **Notes:** Boxing rights are fragmented across promoters (Top Rank, Matchroom, PBC). DAZN has become the largest boxing platform globally. Premium fights still carry PPV surcharges on top of subscriptions. Saudi Arabia has emerged as a major venue host. Turki Alalshikh's Riyadh Season events attract the biggest matchups.

## Cricket

### IPL (Indian Premier League)
- **Category:** Cricket (T20)
- **Season:** March - May
- **Global Platforms:** JioCinema (India, free tier available), Willow TV (US/CA), Sky Sports (UK), Kayo Sports (AU)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - Free on JioCinema (ad-supported)
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £35+/mo via Sky Sports Cricket
- **Key Events:** Opening match, playoffs, eliminator, qualifier, IPL Final
- **Notes:** JioCinema disrupted the market by offering free IPL streaming in India, previously behind a Disney+ Hotstar paywall. IPL is the world's most valuable cricket league and among the most-watched annual sporting events globally. Matches are scheduled for Indian prime time evening slots.

### Cricket World Cup (ICC Events)
- **Category:** Cricket (ODI / T20)
- **Season:** Varies (ODI World Cup every 4 years, T20 World Cup every 2 years)
- **Global Platforms:** Disney+ Hotstar / JioStar (India), Sky Sports (UK), Kayo Sports (AU), Willow TV (US/CA), Fancode (select markets)
- **Cheapest Option:** India - ~$2.50/mo via JioStar
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £35+/mo via Sky Sports
- **Key Events:** Group stage, Super 8s/Super 12s, Semi-finals, Final
- **Notes:** Cricket World Cup viewership is dominated by the Indian subcontinent. T20 World Cup expanding to 20 teams. Free-to-air coverage available in India for ICC events. Time zones significantly affect viewing patterns across markets.

## Tennis

### Grand Slams
- **Category:** Tennis
- **Season:** Year-round (Australian Open Jan, French Open May-Jun, Wimbledon Jun-Jul, US Open Aug-Sep)
- **Global Platforms:** ESPN+ (US), Eurosport/Discovery+ (Europe), Stan Sport (AU), Tennis Channel
- **Cheapest Option:** Free-to-air coverage in select markets (France for Roland Garros on France Télévisions, UK for Wimbledon on BBC)
- **Most Expensive Option:** United States - $110+/yr via ESPN+ / Tennis Channel combined
- **Key Events:** Australian Open, French Open (Roland Garros), Wimbledon, US Open
- **Notes:** Wimbledon remains free-to-air on BBC in the UK. Roland Garros has free-to-air coverage on France Télévisions. Stan Sport carries all four Grand Slams in Australia. ATP/WTA Tour events are spread across multiple platforms. Tennis Channel and Tennis TV provide dedicated coverage outside Grand Slams.

## Rugby

### Six Nations
- **Category:** Rugby Union
- **Season:** February - March
- **Global Platforms:** Peacock (US), ITV/BBC (UK, free-to-air), Stan Sport (AU), Sky Sport (NZ), Virgin Media (IE)
- **Cheapest Option:** United Kingdom / Ireland - Free on BBC and ITV (UK), Virgin Media/RTE (Ireland)
- **Most Expensive Option:** Australia - ~$15/mo via Stan Sport
- **Key Events:** Opening weekend, Super Saturday (final day with all three matches), Grand Slam deciders
- **Notes:** One of the few major sporting events still fully free-to-air in the UK (BBC and ITV share coverage). Six nations participate: England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland, Wales. Triple Crown and Grand Slam are prestigious achievements within the tournament.

### Rugby World Cup
- **Category:** Rugby Union
- **Season:** Every 4 years (September - November)
- **Global Platforms:** Peacock/NBC (US), ITV (UK, free-to-air), Stan Sport (AU), Sky Sport (NZ)
- **Cheapest Option:** United Kingdom - Free on ITV
- **Most Expensive Option:** Australia - ~$15/mo via Stan Sport
- **Key Events:** Pool stages, Quarter-finals, Semi-finals, Bronze Final, Final
- **Notes:** Next Rugby World Cup in 2027 (Australia). Free-to-air coverage protected in the UK. Time zones vary significantly based on host nation. Women's Rugby World Cup growing in prominence and viewership.

## Golf

### Major Championships & PGA Tour
- **Category:** Golf
- **Season:** Year-round (Majors: April, June, July, August)
- **Global Platforms:** ESPN+ / Peacock / CBS (US), Sky Sports Golf (UK), Kayo Sports (AU), DAZN (select markets)
- **Cheapest Option:** Free-to-air coverage of select rounds in multiple markets
- **Most Expensive Option:** United Kingdom - £35+/mo via Sky Sports Golf
- **Key Events:** The Masters (April), PGA Championship (May), US Open (June), The Open Championship (July), Ryder Cup (biennial)
- **Notes:** The Masters is one of the most prestigious events in sports. The Open Championship has free-to-air coverage on BBC in the UK. PGA Tour and LIV Golf merger negotiations continue to reshape the landscape. Ryder Cup (US vs Europe) generates massive viewership every two years.

## Cycling

### Grand Tours & Classics
- **Category:** Cycling
- **Season:** March - October (Grand Tours: May-July/August-September)
- **Global Platforms:** Eurosport/Discovery+ (primary global), Peacock/NBC (US), GCN+ (global), SBS (AU, free-to-air)
- **Cheapest Option:** Australia - Free on SBS for Tour de France
- **Most Expensive Option:** Europe - €8.99/mo via Eurosport/Discovery+
- **Key Events:** Tour de France (July), Giro d'Italia (May), Vuelta a España (Aug-Sep), Paris-Roubaix, Tour of Flanders
- **Notes:** Eurosport/Discovery+ holds primary global rights for all three Grand Tours. GCN+ provides dedicated cycling coverage. Tour de France is by far the most-watched cycling event. Free-to-air coverage varies by country - France Télévisions carries the Tour in France, SBS in Australia.

## Swimming & Aquatics

### World Championships & Olympics
- **Category:** Swimming
- **Season:** Year-round (World Championships typically July-August)
- **Global Platforms:** Olympic Channel, Eurosport/Discovery+ (Europe), NBC/Peacock (US), local Olympic broadcasters
- **Cheapest Option:** Free-to-air coverage during Olympics and major events in most countries
- **Most Expensive Option:** Minimal standalone cost - typically bundled with broader sports packages
- **Key Events:** World Aquatics Championships, Olympic Games, World Cup series
- **Notes:** Swimming viewership peaks massively during Olympic Games. Most major swimming events have free-to-air components. World Aquatics Championships held annually (short course and long course alternate). Olympic channel provides year-round coverage of aquatic sports.

## Athletics (Track & Field)

### World Championships & Diamond League
- **Category:** Athletics
- **Season:** May - October (outdoor), January - March (indoor)
- **Global Platforms:** Olympic Channel, Eurosport/Discovery+ (Europe), NBC/Peacock (US), BBC (UK, select events free-to-air)
- **Cheapest Option:** Free-to-air coverage on BBC (UK) and public broadcasters for major events
- **Most Expensive Option:** Typically bundled with broader sports packages at no additional cost
- **Key Events:** World Athletics Championships, Olympic Games, Diamond League Finals, World Indoor Championships
- **Notes:** Diamond League is the premier annual series with meets across global cities. World Athletics Championships held biennially. Athletics is the centerpiece of the Olympic Games. Marathon Majors (Boston, London, Berlin, Chicago, NYC, Tokyo) have dedicated coverage.
