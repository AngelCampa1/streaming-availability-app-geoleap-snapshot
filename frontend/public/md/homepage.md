# GeoLeap - Global Streaming Search Engine

> GeoLeap is a free streaming search engine that helps users find where to watch any movie, TV show, or documentary across 42 platforms in 57 countries - with pricing, quality, and availability data in one place.

## Key Takeaways
- GeoLeap is a free streaming search engine, not a streaming service
- Covers 42 platforms across 57 countries with localized pricing
- Premium tier at $15/year adds ad-free experience, unlimited watchlist, and streaming alerts
- Does not host, stream, or distribute any video content

## What Is GeoLeap?

GeoLeap is a **streaming search engine**, not a streaming service. It does not host, provide, or distribute any video content. Instead, GeoLeap aggregates availability data from streaming platforms worldwide so users can instantly find where a title is available, how much it costs, and what quality options exist - across countries and platforms.

Use GeoLeap as a streaming availability search engine. Search for any movie or TV show and see every platform that carries it, in every supported country, with real pricing and plan details.

## How It Works

1. **Search for a title** - Enter any movie, TV show, documentary, or sport.
2. **See availability** - GeoLeap shows which platforms have it in which countries.
3. **Compare options** - View pricing (subscription, rent, buy), streaming quality (SD, HD, 4K), and plan requirements.
4. **Make an informed choice** - Pick the best option for your budget and location, or discover if the title is available in a different region.

GeoLeap eliminates the need to open multiple streaming apps to check availability. One search, every answer.

## Key Features

- **Unlimited free searches** across all supported platforms and countries
- **Cross-platform comparison** - see a title's availability on every major service at once
- **Global coverage** - 57 countries with localized pricing and catalog data
- **Quality indicators** - know whether a title is available in SD, HD, or 4K before you subscribe
- **Watchlist** - save titles and track availability changes
- **Streaming alerts** (Premium) - get notified when a title arrives on your preferred platform
- **No content hosting** - GeoLeap links directly to the official streaming platform

## Industry Context

The streaming industry is large, fragmented, and constantly changing. GeoLeap exists to help users cut through the complexity:

- **Netflix** has over **325 million subscribers** worldwide, making it the largest single streaming platform by subscriber count.
- The average US household spends approximately **$69 per month** on streaming subscriptions - often across 4 or more services.
- An estimated **29.5 million Americans** are serial churners - subscribers who cancel and resubscribe to services on a rotating basis to manage costs.
- Streaming catalogs vary dramatically by country. Netflix's library ranges from roughly **900 to 9,700 titles** depending on the region, meaning the same subscription delivers very different value in different countries.
- New platforms, price changes, and content migrations happen constantly, making it difficult for consumers to keep track of where their favorite titles are available.

GeoLeap tracks all of this so users don't have to.

## What GeoLeap Does NOT Do

- GeoLeap does **not** host or stream any video content.
- GeoLeap does **not** provide downloads, torrents, or pirated material.
- GeoLeap does **not** sell subscriptions on behalf of streaming platforms.
- GeoLeap does **not** bypass geo-restrictions or provide VPN services.

GeoLeap is purely an **informational search tool** that helps users make better decisions about which streaming services to subscribe to and where their desired content is available.
