# GeoLeap - Pricing

> GeoLeap is free to use for streaming content search. Premium adds convenience features like ad-free browsing, unlimited watchlist, and streaming alerts for $15/year.

## Key Takeaways
- Core streaming search is completely free with no account required
- Free tier includes unlimited searches across 42 platforms and 57 countries
- Premium costs $15/year, about $2/month, and adds ad-free, unlimited watchlist, and alerts
- 30-day free trial included with Premium

## Overview

GeoLeap is a streaming search engine that helps users find where to watch movies, TV shows, and documentaries across 42 platforms in 57 countries. The core search functionality is free for everyone. A Premium tier adds convenience and power-user features at a low annual price.

## Free Tier

The Free tier gives users full access to GeoLeap's core search engine and content library at no cost.

**Included:**

- Unlimited searches: Search for any movie, TV show, or documentary with no daily or monthly caps
- Full platform and country coverage: See availability across all 42 supported platforms in 57 countries
- Pricing and quality data: View subscription costs, rental and purchase prices, and streaming quality (SD, HD, 4K) for every result
- 10-item watchlist: Save up to 10 titles to track
- All editorial content: Full access to guides, comparisons, glossary, blog, sports coverage, genre recommendations, and how-to-watch articles
- Basic search filters: Filter by platform, country, and content type
- Ad-supported: Free tier is supported by non-intrusive advertising

**Cost: $0**

## Premium Tier

Premium removes ads and unlocks power-user features for a single annual payment.

**Included (everything in Free, plus):**

- Ad-free experience: No advertisements anywhere on the platform
- Unlimited watchlist: Save as many titles as you want with no cap
- Streaming alerts: Get notified when a title becomes available on a specific platform or in a specific country, or when a title is about to leave a platform
- Advanced search filters: Additional filtering options for more precise results
- Priority support: Faster response times from the GeoLeap support team
- 30-day free trial: Try Premium risk-free; cancel anytime during the trial

**Cost: $15/year** (about $2/month)

## Value Proposition

- $15/year is about $2/month. That is less than the cost of a single streaming add-on channel.
- The average US household spends approximately $69/month on streaming across multiple services. GeoLeap helps users find the best value across all of them.
- An estimated 29.5 million Americans churn between streaming services to manage costs. GeoLeap makes it easy to see exactly which services carry the content you want before you subscribe.
- Premium pays for itself if it saves you from even one month of an unnecessary streaming subscription.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is GeoLeap really free to use?

Yes. The core streaming search engine is completely free with no account required. You can search for any title and see full availability, pricing, and quality data across all supported platforms and countries at no cost. The Free tier is ad-supported.

### What does Premium add?

Premium removes ads and adds convenience features: unlimited watchlist (instead of 10 items), streaming alerts that notify you when titles become available or are about to leave a platform, advanced search filters, and priority support.

### Is there a free trial for Premium?

Yes. Premium includes a 30-day free trial. You can cancel anytime during the trial and will not be charged.

### Can I cancel Premium anytime?

Yes. You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time. If you cancel, you retain Premium access until the end of your current billing period.

### Does GeoLeap stream content?

No. GeoLeap is a search engine, not a streaming service. It does not host, provide, or distribute any video content. When you find a title, GeoLeap links you directly to the official streaming platform where it is available.

### Why is Premium inexpensive?

GeoLeap's mission is to help everyone navigate the fragmented streaming market. Premium is priced to be accessible. At about $2/month, it costs less than almost any streaming add-on. The low price reflects that GeoLeap is a search and discovery tool, not a content provider.

### Do I need an account to search?

No. You can use GeoLeap's search engine without creating an account. An account is only needed if you want to use the watchlist feature or subscribe to Premium.
