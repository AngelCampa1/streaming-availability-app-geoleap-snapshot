## Fixes Applied in This Session

### 1. Sitemap: added missing pages (P0)

**File:** `frontend/src/app/sitemap.ts`

Added three pages that existed but were unknown to Google:
- `/faq` (priority 0.6): FAQ schema content, AI citation target
- `/help` (priority 0.5): trust signal
- `/about/authors` (priority 0.7): critical E-E-A-T, author credibility

GSC URL inspection confirmed these three as "URL is unknown to Google" with PAGE_FETCH_STATE_UNSPECIFIED.

### 2. Sitemap: removed phantom genre/year filter URLs (P0)

**File:** `frontend/src/app/sitemap.ts`

Removed `generateGenreSitemapPages()` and `generateYearSitemapPages()` calls from `generateDynamicContentPages()`. These generated ~500+ sitemap entries for URLs like `/movies/genre/action` and `/movies/year/2024`, but no corresponding route files (`page.tsx`) exist. These would have served 404s to Google, consuming crawl budget and signaling low site quality.

### 3. Comparison page titles: added year bracket (P1)

**File:** `frontend/src/app/(marketing)/compare/[slug]/page.tsx`

Updated `generateMetadata` to append `[2026]` to comparison titles that don't already include the year. Before: `"Netflix vs Hulu: Which Streaming Service Is Right for You?"`. After: `"Netflix vs Hulu: Which Streaming Service Is Right for You? [2026]"`. Year brackets improve CTR by signaling freshness in SERPs.

### 4. Added missing alex-chen author (P0)

**File:** `frontend/src/data/authors.ts`

13 blog posts referenced `authorSlug: 'alex-chen'` but the author didn't exist in `authors.ts`. This meant those 13 posts:
- Showed no author byline on the page
- Fell back to Organization (instead of Person) in Article schema
- Had no E-E-A-T signal for nearly 20% of blog content

Added Alex Chen as "Streaming Data Analyst & Consumer Tech Reporter" with credentials, expertise, and social links matching the style of existing authors.

### 5. Added "Last updated" date to blog template (P1)

**File:** `frontend/src/app/(marketing)/blog/[slug]/page.tsx`

Blog was the only pSEO template without a visible "Last updated" date. Added conditional rendering of `post.updatedAt` (only shows when different from `publishedAt`).

### 6. Shortened homepage title to 60 chars (P2)

**File:** `frontend/src/app/layout.tsx`

Before: `"Find Where to Watch Any Show - 30+ Services, 50+ Countries | GeoLeap"` (68 chars)
After: `"Find Where to Watch - 30+ Services, 50+ Countries | GeoLeap"` (60 chars)

### 7. Added @id to Person schema for E-E-A-T entity resolution (P2)

**Files:** `frontend/src/lib/seo/schema-markup.ts`, `frontend/src/app/(marketing)/guides/[slug]/page.tsx`

Added `@id` property to Person author in Article schema, matching the author page URL. This creates a linked entity chain: Article → Person (author) ↔ Person (author page) → Organization (GeoLeap). Also added `@id` to Organization fallback in guide schema for consistency.

### 8. Updated llms.txt and llms-full.txt with correct counts

**Files:** `frontend/public/llms.txt`, `frontend/public/llms-full.txt`

- Blog articles: 52+ → 63+
- Authors: 3 → 4
- Added Alex Chen to authors section

### 9. Verified speakable CSS selectors (P2, RESOLVED)

`.bluf-summary` class confirmed present in 11 pSEO templates (blog, compare, compare/in, platforms, countries, platform/genres, sports, how-to-watch, glossary, genres/country, genres). No DOM issues.

## Open Issues

### P0: Comparison page flagged noindex in GSC

`/compare/netflix-vs-disney-plus-global` shows "Excluded by 'noindex' tag" in GSC URL inspection, but the live page serves `index, follow`. Likely stale GSC data from before the slug was added to the comparisons array.

**Action required (not code):** Manually request re-indexing via GSC → URL Inspection → "Request Indexing".

### P1: Statistics cited without sources

Blog posts contain specific statistics with no source attribution:
- "streaming-price-increases-2026": $255B global content spending, Netflix 108% price increase, 85% broadband penetration, no sources
- Pattern repeats across other high-impression blog posts

**Impact:** Unsourced statistics reduce AI citation probability by 40%. Tier-1 sources (.gov, .edu, industry reports) give 132% boost.

**Action required:** Add inline citations to top 10 blog posts by impressions. Start with: fast-channels-free-tv-2026, streaming-price-increases-2026, streaming-profitability-scorecard-2026, best-netflix-library-by-country.

### P1: Thin comparison pages (~800-1000 words)

Top comparison pages run 800-1,000 words vs. the 2,000-3,000 words that top-ranking comparison content typically has. 58 comparisons total, only 8 have custom seoTitle, FAQ count ranges from 1 to 5 (avg 2.7), comparison points range from 4 to 7.

**Action required:** Expand comparison data to include: per-plan pricing breakdown, feature matrix, regional differences, 5-8 FAQs (currently avg 2.7).

### P2: Sitemap may have duplicate blog entries

WebFetch detected what appeared to be a blog entry appearing twice with different dates. Verify `generateBlogGuidesGlossaryPages()` produces unique URLs.
