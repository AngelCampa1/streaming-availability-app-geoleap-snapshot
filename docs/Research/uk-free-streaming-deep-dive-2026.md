# UK Free Streaming Deep Dive 2026: BBC iPlayer, ITVX, All4, My5

*Published March 2026*

---

## Overview of the UK Free Streaming Ecosystem

The United Kingdom has one of the world's most developed free streaming ecosystems. Four major commercial and public broadcaster catch-up services — BBC iPlayer, ITVX, All4, and My5 — provide access to the full output of the UK's primary broadcast channels at no cost to the user beyond an internet connection (and in the BBC's case, a TV licence if watching live).

These services are genuinely world-class. BBC iPlayer in particular is regularly cited as a global benchmark for public broadcaster digital strategy. The depth of archive content, the quality of original productions, and the ease of use across devices are consistently high. What makes them particularly painful to lose is that they are free at home — there is no premium tier or paid equivalent that unlocks international access.

For the estimated 5–6 million British citizens living abroad, these four services represent a daily connection to home culture that simply disappears the moment they board a flight.

## BBC iPlayer

### What It Is

BBC iPlayer is the on-demand and live streaming service of the British Broadcasting Corporation. It carries the full output of BBC One, BBC Two, BBC Three (online only), BBC Four, BBC News, CBBC, CBeebies, and BBC Radio, along with an extensive archive of BBC content dating back decades in some cases.

### Content Highlights

**Drama:** Programmes like Sherlock, Luther, Line of Duty, Happy Valley, Fleabag, Normal People, and The Bodyguard were all major international cultural moments that premiered or were exclusively streamed on iPlayer. The archive is extensive.

**Comedy:** Fawlty Towers, Blackadder, Only Fools and Horses, Inside No. 9, Ghosts (UK), and a continuous stream of new BBC comedy. Some classic BBC comedy is among the most-watched British TV globally.

**Documentary:** Blue Planet, Planet Earth, Human Planet, and subsequent natural history programmes are BBC productions. David Attenborough's documentary output is substantially held on iPlayer before international licensing deals move it to other platforms.

**News and current affairs:** BBC News 24 live, Newsnight, Question Time, Panorama, and local news programming.

**Sport:** Match of the Day (Premier League highlights), some BBC Sport live events, Wimbledon, Six Nations rugby, and the London Marathon. Sport availability on iPlayer is more limited than on commercial services, but flagship BBC Sport events are significant.

**Children's:** CBBC and CBeebies content. British expat families with young children frequently cite losing CBeebies — with programmes like Hey Duggee, Go Jetters, and Bluey (Australia/BBC co-production) — as a major pain point.

### TV Licence Requirement

The BBC TV licence is required for watching BBC programmes live as they are broadcast, and also for using BBC iPlayer (for catch-up and on-demand content, following rule changes in 2016). The licence costs approximately £169.50/year as of 2025.

Technically, British nationals abroad who maintain a UK address and hold a TV licence are entitled to use iPlayer. In practice, iPlayer's geo-restriction prevents access outside the UK regardless of licence status. This is a source of particular frustration for licence payers who travel abroad.

### Geo-Restriction Abroad

BBC iPlayer detects your location by IP address and blocks access if you appear to be outside the United Kingdom. The error message ("BBC iPlayer only works in the UK") is one of the most recognisable geo-restriction messages in the world.

The BBC's iPlayer geo-restriction has been in place since the service launched. The BBC's position is that it is licensed to broadcast in the UK, and international streaming rights for many programmes are sold separately to local broadcasters, meaning the BBC cannot legally stream them to international audiences.

Smart DNS services that make a device appear to be located in the UK resolve this restriction for British expats and travellers.

## ITVX

### What It Is

ITVX (formerly ITV Hub) is the streaming service of ITV, the UK's largest commercial broadcaster. ITV holds broadcast rights to a significant portion of commercially important UK TV including long-running soaps, reality programming, drama, and some live sport.

### Content Highlights

**Soaps:** Coronation Street and Emmerdale are two of the UK's longest-running and most-watched TV programmes. Coronation Street (since 1960) and Emmerdale (since 1972) both broadcast multiple episodes per week. For British expats who have followed these programmes for years or decades, losing access is particularly disruptive.

**Reality/entertainment:** The X Factor, I'm a Celebrity Get Me Out of Here!, Love Island, Britain's Got Talent, and The Chase. Love Island in particular has significant international social media engagement, making the inability to watch live episodes while they air frustrating for British expats who follow the social media conversation in real time.

**Drama:** Broadchurch, Trigger Point, Karen Pirie, The Tower, and ITV's ongoing drama commissions. ITV has historically been a strong producer of crime drama and prestige limited series.

**Sport:** ITV holds some football broadcast rights (Euro international, FA Cup, and some Champions League coverage is split between ITV and BBC). ITV Sport coverage is free on ITVX.

### Free vs Premium

ITVX operates a freemium model:
- **ITVX Free:** Full catch-up catalogue for all ITV channels, available with ads
- **ITVX Premium:** Ad-free viewing, access to ITVX originals that are exclusive to Premium, and BritBox integration (a separate service of classic British TV)

Premium pricing is approximately £3.99/month or £39.99/year.

### Geo-Restriction

ITVX is geo-restricted to the United Kingdom. Access from outside the UK (including EU countries, Australia, the US) requires Smart DNS or a VPN configured to appear as a UK IP address.

## All4 (Channel 4)

### What It Is

All4 is the streaming service of Channel 4, the UK's publicly owned but commercially funded broadcaster. Channel 4 occupies a distinctive cultural position in UK broadcasting — it has a public service remit that includes support for independent production companies, representation of minority audiences, and alternative perspectives. This mandate has historically made Channel 4 the home of boundary-pushing British TV.

### Content Highlights

**Drama and film:** Channel 4's film arm (Film4) co-produces or finances a significant proportion of British cinema. Films like Trainspotting, Slumdog Millionaire, 28 Days Later, The Favourite, and many others have Film4 in their credits. A substantial portion of this library is available on All4.

**Walter Presents:** One of All4's most distinctive features. Walter Presents is a curated international drama strand that brings subtitled European and world drama to British audiences. Series from Germany (Dark, Das Boot), France (Spiral/Engrenages), Italy, Scandinavia, Israel, and beyond are available here, often before Netflix or Amazon make international drama mainstream.

**Comedy:** Channel 4 has a strong comedy heritage — Father Ted (though now on multiple platforms), Peep Show, Fresh Meat, Derry Girls, Fargo (UK broadcast rights), and The IT Crowd.

**News and current affairs:** Channel 4 News, Dispatches, and Unreported World represent distinctive investigative and international journalism not available elsewhere.

**Reality:** Big Brother (revived on Channel 4), Gogglebox, The Great British Bake Off (now on Channel 4 after moving from BBC), and Taskmaster.

### Geo-Restriction

All4 is geo-restricted to the United Kingdom. The service is available for free with ads and does not require a subscription. Its content is simply unavailable outside UK IP address ranges.

## My5 (Channel 5)

### What It Is

My5 is the streaming service of Channel 5, owned by Paramount (rebranded from Viacom). It is the smallest of the four major UK FTA broadcaster catch-up services but carries content that has a dedicated audience.

### Content Highlights

**Reality and lifestyle:** Channel 5 is known for property shows (Channel 4 competition shows having moved, Channel 5 has built its own slate), benefits and housing documentaries, and accessible factual programming.

**US content:** Paramount ownership means Channel 5 sometimes holds UK broadcast rights to Paramount/CBS network content.

**Drama:** Chanel 5 has invested more in original UK drama in recent years, with series like The Drowning, All Creatures Great and Small (revived co-production with PBS Masterpiece), and crime drama.

**Soaps:** Neighbours (the Australian soap opera) had a long history on Channel 5 before ending. It was revived for Amazon Freevee but Channel 5's archive of this content matters to long-time fans.

### Geo-Restriction

My5 is geo-restricted to the United Kingdom. Free with ads.

## What Expats Lose When Leaving the UK

When a British citizen moves abroad, they instantly lose access to:

1. Every episode of EastEnders, Coronation Street, Emmerdale, and Hollyoaks
2. BBC iPlayer's entire archive of British drama, comedy, and documentary
3. Live BBC, ITV, Channel 4, and Channel 5 television
4. Match of the Day and other BBC/ITV sports programming
5. The Great British Bake Off as it airs
6. All4's Walter Presents international drama catalogue
7. Ability to keep up with British reality TV (Love Island, I'm a Celebrity) as episodes air
8. BBC Radio live streams through iPlayer
9. Free-to-access content they have paid for via TV licence

The aggregate cost of this lost content in emotional and cultural terms is significant. The financial cost, were legal international equivalents available, would be substantial — these services are free in the UK.

## How to Access UK Streaming Abroad

The practical solution for British expats is Smart DNS configured to present a UK IP address to streaming platforms. GeoLeap's UK profile routes the authentication check for BBC iPlayer, ITVX, All4, and My5 through UK servers, making all four services accessible from anywhere in the world.

Configuration is straightforward: change the DNS server address on your device or router to GeoLeap's servers, select the UK profile, and all four services work as normal. Smart TVs, streaming sticks, games consoles, phones, tablets, and laptops are all supported via router-level configuration.

The main limitation is that accounts may be needed — BBC iPlayer requires a free account (with a UK postcode during registration), ITVX requires a free account, All4 requires a free account, and My5 requires a free account. All of these can be created with a UK postcode.
